Coin Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

FlyLive uses two virtual currencies — Coins and Diamonds. Both are platform tokens used for in-app features such as sending gifts, participating in Lucky Draw, and engaging with the community. Neither currency has any real-world monetary value.

This page explains how coins and diamonds work, how coin grants are allocated, and what you can expect from the system.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Coins?

Coins are a virtual platform token used to send gifts to other users, participate in Lucky Draw, and engage with platform features. Coins have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for real-world currency, goods, or services under any circumstances.

What are Diamonds?

Diamonds are a separate virtual token that eligible agency members may receive. Diamonds can be converted to Coins at rates set by the platform. Like Coins, Diamonds have no monetary value and cannot be converted to real-world currency.

How do I get Coins?

Submit a coin grant request from the Coins section of the app. Our team reviews your account and approves or denies the request based on your platform engagement. Coin grants are free — no payment of any kind is required or accepted.

What engagement signals are considered?

Grant decisions are based on factors including account age, login frequency, community participation, and overall platform activity. The FlyLive team evaluates each request at its sole discretion.

Can my request be denied?

Yes. The FlyLive team may approve, deny, or adjust any coin grant request at any time, with or without providing a reason. Submitting a request does not guarantee any coins will be granted.

Can I buy Coins?

No. FlyLive does not sell coins or diamonds for real money. Coins are granted based on platform engagement only. No payment is ever required or accepted for virtual currency.

Are Coins refundable?

Coin allocations are final and non-reversible. Because no real money is involved in any coin transaction, there is nothing to refund.

Can the platform change or remove my Coin balance?

Yes. FlyLive reserves the right to adjust, revoke, or expire virtual currency balances at any time, including for violations of our Terms of Service. Because coins have no monetary value, no compensation is owed for balance adjustments.

What happens to my Coins if I delete my account?

When you delete your account, all coin and diamond balances are permanently forfeited. Transaction records may be retained for platform audit and fraud prevention purposes even after account deletion.

What is Lucky Draw?

Lucky Draw is an entertainment feature where you spend virtual coins for a chance to receive a larger virtual coin amount. The odds for each outcome are shown before you participate. All prizes are virtual coins. No real-world prizes or monetary value of any kind is awarded.
