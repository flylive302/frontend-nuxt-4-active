Child Safety Standards

FlyLive's commitment to protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation

Our Commitment

FlyLive is committed to creating a safe environment for all users and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We actively work to prevent, detect, and respond to any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or exploitative behavior on our platform.

Published Standards Against CSAE

FlyLive maintains the following standards to combat child sexual abuse and exploitation:

  • Zero Tolerance Policy: Any content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children — including grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or sharing of CSAM — is strictly prohibited on FlyLive.
  • Age Requirement: FlyLive requires all users to be at least 18 years of age. Users who are found to be underage will have their accounts removed.
  • Proactive Moderation: Our moderation team actively monitors content and user interactions to identify and remove any potential CSAE-related activity.
  • Immediate Action: Upon obtaining knowledge of any CSAM on our platform, we take immediate action to remove the content, disable the offending account, and report to the relevant authorities.

In-App Reporting Mechanism

FlyLive provides in-app mechanisms for users to report child safety concerns:

  • User Reporting: Users can report any user or content directly within the app using the report function available on every profile and in every audio room.
  • Report Categories: Our reporting system includes specific categories for child safety concerns to ensure these reports are prioritized and handled with urgency.
  • Confidential Handling: All child safety reports are treated with the highest level of confidentiality and urgency.

CSAM Prevention and Response

FlyLive takes the following measures to address child sexual abuse material:

  • We prohibit the upload, sharing, or distribution of any CSAM on our platform.
  • When CSAM is identified, it is immediately removed and the account is permanently banned.
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies and report CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant local authorities as required by law.
  • We retain relevant evidence as required by applicable laws to assist in investigations.

Compliance with Laws

FlyLive complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

  • Reporting obligations under national and international law
  • Cooperation with law enforcement investigations
  • Data preservation requirements for child safety investigations
  • Age verification and restriction requirements

Child Safety Point of Contact

For child safety concerns, reports, or inquiries, please contact our designated child safety team:

  • Email: support@flyliveapp.com
  • In-App: Use the report function within FlyLive to flag any child safety concerns directly

Our child safety point of contact is ready and able to address any CSAM prevention inquiries and ensure compliance with our child safety standards.

User Education

FlyLive is committed to educating our community about child safety. We encourage all users to:

  • Report any suspicious behavior or content involving minors immediately
  • Never share personal information with unknown users
  • Be vigilant about potential grooming or exploitative behavior
