Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

FlyLive ("we", "our", "us") operates the FlyLive mobile application and website (flyliveapp.com). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service.

By using FlyLive, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name and profile information
  • Phone number and country
  • Email address (optional, via social login)
  • Date of birth and gender
  • Profile photos and room images
  • Transaction and virtual currency data

3. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time through the app's profile settings. Upon deletion, your account enters a 30-day grace period during which you can contact support to cancel. After 30 days, your personal data is permanently deleted from our servers, except for transaction records retained for legal compliance.

4. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: support@flyliveapp.com

Note: This is a preliminary privacy policy. A comprehensive version covering all data practices, third-party services, and international data transfers will be published before the app's public release on the Google Play Store.
